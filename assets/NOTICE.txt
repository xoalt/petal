Petal is an unofficial Linux port/reimplementation of Tangerine for macOS,
by Leap Studio: https://tangerineformac.com/

Petal is independently written. No original source repository was supplied
or verified, and this is not an official release or a source-code fork.
Original Tangerine icons, screenshots, and demo video are not redistributed.

The Petal rosette logo was created specifically for this project using the
built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, then exported at app-icon size.
The logo is included with this project under the MIT license to the extent
applicable. No claim of exclusive rights in AI-generated output is made.
See docs/branding.md for the design prompt and generation details.
